# Meridian Industries Limited — Annual Report FY2025 (Excerpt)

## Chairman's Statement

Financial year 2025 was a year of disciplined growth for Meridian Industries. Consolidated
revenue rose to 48,200 million rupees, an increase of 14 percent over the 42,300 million
rupees reported in FY2024. Growth was led by the Industrial Automation segment, which
benefited from strong order inflows in the second half of the year.

## Financial Highlights

- Consolidated revenue: 48,200 million rupees (FY2024: 42,300 million rupees)
- Earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation (EBITDA): 9,150 million rupees
- EBITDA margin: 19.0 percent, up from 17.8 percent in the prior year
- Profit after tax: 5,420 million rupees, up 21 percent year on year
- Basic earnings per share: 27.4 rupees (FY2024: 22.6 rupees)
- The Board has recommended a dividend of 6.50 rupees per equity share.

## Segment Performance

The Industrial Automation segment contributed 27,900 million rupees of revenue, or roughly
58 percent of the consolidated total. The Energy Systems segment contributed 14,100 million
rupees, and the remaining 6,200 million rupees came from the Services segment. Operating
margin in Industrial Automation expanded to 22 percent, aided by a richer product mix and
lower input costs in the second half.

## Capital Expenditure and Balance Sheet

Capital expenditure for the year was 3,800 million rupees, directed mainly toward capacity
expansion at the Pune and Halol facilities. Net debt at the close of the year stood at
4,600 million rupees, giving a net debt to EBITDA ratio of 0.50 times, comfortably within the
Board's stated ceiling of 1.5 times. Cash and cash equivalents were 7,900 million rupees.

## Risks and Outlook

Management notes that the principal risks for the coming year are input cost volatility,
particularly in semiconductors and copper, and foreign exchange exposure on imported
components, of which roughly 35 percent are sourced from outside India. For FY2026,
management guides to revenue growth in the range of 11 to 13 percent, with EBITDA margin
expected to hold between 18.5 and 19.5 percent.
